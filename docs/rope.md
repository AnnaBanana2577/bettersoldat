# The rope

The gear that replaces the jets, played with the same key. A Worms/Liero line the
soldier flings at the aim; it holds on the first poly it reaches, and from then on
it is a way up, a way down, a swing, and a slingshot — all of it on the rope key,
the up and down keys, and left and right.

It is experimental, and off unless the server allows it: sv_rope 1 on a dedicated
server, or Rope On on the main menu's Local Play page. Off, everyone has jets and the
weapons menu shows no Boots row; on, a player picks the rope there (cl_player_gear).

## The throw

Hold the rope key (the jet key): the rope leaves the hand, flying at the aim the way
a LAW rocket flies — at the LAW's pace at first, losing a little of its speed every
tick and falling under gravity at the end. Let the key go before it holds and the
rope retracts. The rope is 800 long at most: a throw past its reach, or off the map,
drops it.

## The hold

The rope holds on the first poly the soldier can stand on. Hung, it is a rod of the
length it flew: taut, nothing moves along it, so the soldier swings under it freely.
Pulled past its length the rope gives — up to a fifth of itself — stretching like a
real line and pulling back, the bounce settling. A hard pull bounces instead of
snapping.

## The swing

Left and right swing the hang along its arc and roll the soldier up into the
cannonball. The roll plays slowly, so it reads, and every tick of holding gathers
momentum along the arc. Held with the up or the down key, the sideways push joins
the climb or the payout — climbing and paying out at an angle. The rope key pressed
mid-swing cuts the rope and leaves the soldier flying on the swing — the slingshot:
swing up, cut at the top, and the arc throws them.

## The climb

The up key climbs the rope to its anchor: a slow pull that builds — a tenth of the
rope's pace at first, a hundredth more every tick it is held, a fifth of it at most.
The pull is real motion, not a reel: the climb's momentum is the soldier's own, so
the rope key pressed mid-climb cuts the rope and lets them fly on it, and the rope
let go at the top keeps the pull — there the rope lets go by itself and stands its
owner where it held.

## The payout

The down key feeds the rope out again, at the pace the climb reels it in — the one
ramp serves both, so what goes up comes down at the same speed — and only as far as
the rope was when it grabbed: the climb's slack back out, never more rope than the
throw made. To hang lower than the throw reached, throw again.

## Around corners

Held, the rope cannot pass through the polys between its anchor and its owner: it
catches on the first corner its line crosses and winds around it, the wound part
held around the solid while the rest hangs from the corner. Swung back until the
straight line from the point before the corner is clear again and the rope reaches,
the corner lets go. A rope hung over an edge drapes over it, and drags along it
when climbed or paid out.

## The cut

The rope key pressed while it holds cuts it, the momentum kept — swing, climb, or
hang. The knife, the thrown knife, the LAW, the M79 and the Barrett cut a rope their
bullet crosses; every other bullet passes it by.
